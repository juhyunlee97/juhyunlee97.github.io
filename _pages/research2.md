Wearable devices are not only effective for digital healthcare, but also hold great potential in broader applications such as human-machine interfaces and entertainment. <br>
For wearable systems to become truly ubiquitous in everyday life, they must be lightweight, compact, and fully wireless ([Advanced Electronic Materials, 2025](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/aelm.202400884)). <br>
With this in mind, I have developed a skin-conformable wireless EMG patch and successfully demonstrated its use in controlling an exoskeleton system ([Science Advances, 2024](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adk5260)). <br>
More recently, I have become increasingly interested in the convergence of wearable technologies and artificial intelligence, which I believe will define a new paradigm in the field.
